## Country-condition doctrine

Research the real conditions in {{COUNTRY_NAME}} and adapt the implementation around them. Do not convert that research into a country veto or claim that people elsewhere must satisfy a safety test before a civic library may exist.

Document uncertainty, preserve sources, reduce needless exposure, and leave consequential publication decisions with the local human maintainer.

The kernel is a library, not a central authority. The people who operate this independent project decide how it is used and remain responsible for its claims, publication choices, and relationship with its community.

## Cold-state promise

At every stopping point, leave the repository so another person or capable coding agent can continue without reconstructing hidden context:

- Changes are committed in coherent units.
- `TASKS.md` tells the truth about what is next.
- `DECISIONS.md` preserves non-obvious calls and their reasons.
- Coverage claims match independently verified sources.
- Private recipes remain outside the public tree.

The repository includes its own validator and static reference-site builder under `tools/`. A preview is always marked `noindex`; a publication artifact requires the human-controlled `--publication-approved` invocation and passes only records backed by confirmed jurisdictions, active governing bodies, and verified sources.
