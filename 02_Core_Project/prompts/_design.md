# Visual Design Spec — Z-SPAN Studio Outputs

Prepended to Studio-generated visual outputs (infographic, video cover,
etc.) when their front-matter sets `include_design: true`. Keeps the
visual look consistent with the broadcast page's "Kingman Insight" dark
aesthetic — a deliberate choice so Studio outputs *belong* on the page
rather than feeling like a foreign light-mode artifact pasted onto a dark
viewer.

The text below the marker is what the bridge actually sends. Edit it to
refine the design direction; keep it tight (Studio prompts have limited
room and the design block is *prepended* to the per-output instructions,
not the only content).

The constant prefix here is intentional: every visual Studio output that
will be embedded on the broadcast page should share this register so the
viewer experience is coherent.

---

## DESIGN BLOCK — sent to Studio

**Visual aesthetic for this output: Z-SPAN dark civic-broadcast.**

Background: deep near-black (approximately `#0E0E10` — like a premium
financial-news broadcast set, not pure black). Body text and primary
content: light off-white / warm neutral on the dark background
(approximately `#E4E4E7`). Accent palette used sparingly for headers,
key data points, vote outcomes, and emphasis: civic green (`#22C55E`)
and civic blue (`#3B82F6`). Typography: clean modern sans-serif with
strong hierarchy via size and weight only — no decorative typefaces.

Aesthetic reference: ultra-dark professional civic broadcast — the
visual register of C-SPAN, Bloomberg Terminal, or a serious municipal
information channel. Authoritative, calm, source-grounded. The output
will be embedded on a dark broadcast page; it must feel native to that
surface, not pasted in from elsewhere.

Avoid: cyberpunk styling, neon accents, glitch effects, cartoonish
illustrations, mascots, light or cheerful backgrounds, pure-black voids
that swallow contrast, decorative gradients, busy patterns, or any look
more suited to consumer marketing than civic record.
